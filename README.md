The GB model flips the constraint. You buy traffic, generate as many endpoints as you like, and never think about IP counts — which also means you never need the app. Sessions can be sticky or rotating, and the 180-day validity matters if your workload comes in unpredictable waves.

## Which one should you buy?

Match the billing model to the shape of your workload, not to its size:

- **Long sessions on the same identity** — account management, cart or login workflows, anything where dropping the IP mid-task breaks the job. That's an IP-based package.
- **Traffic-heavy jobs with unpredictable page weight** — the unlimited bandwidth per IP removes the counter-watching. Also IP-based.
- **Rotation-heavy, request-light work** — ad verification, geo checks, price sampling, API polling, cloud functions. GB-based is cheaper and doesn't require the desktop app.
- **Bursty projects with no fixed monthly rhythm** — GB-based, because the 180-day clock is generous and you aren't forced to burn credits in a 30-day window.
- **Both at once** — a bundle.

One practical limit to know before you buy: 9Proxy's acceptable-use terms have moved away from supporting media streaming on IP-based plans, according to ProxyLook's review. If streaming was your reason, confirm current terms with support first.
